Why the bar is opaque

It used to be 82% over the desktop. A bar floats on a wallpaper nobody who built it has seen, so a palette's contrast guarantees - which are computed against an opaque background - do not hold for anything painted on it. Sweeping every grey wallpaper rather than just white and black, the 1px chip outline bottoms out at 1.77:1 at 82%, against the 3:1 that WCAG asks of a boundary. It clears that only at full opacity, and then only just: 3.00 to 3.04:1 across the fifteen.

So the bar is opaque, and the text is safe for a separate reason: every readout sits inside an opaque chip, which puts it back under the palette's own guarantees whatever is behind. e2e/contrast.spec.ts holds that by measuring this page over a white and a black backdrop and requiring the readings to be identical, not merely passing.
